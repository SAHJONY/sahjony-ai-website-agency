Straight answers

Before you get started.

Is this a DIY website builder?

No. FrontDesk Agents is done-for-you. You give us the business information and direction; we design, build, configure, and launch the system for you.

How quickly can my website go live?

Most standard websites can move from approved content to launch within days. Larger stores, integrations, and custom operational workflows may require more time.

Can the site work in English and Spanish?

Yes. Bilingual delivery is available, and Ava can communicate with visitors in more than 100 languages.

Do I own my domain and business accounts?

Yes. Your domain and connected payment or business accounts remain yours. We configure and manage the technical experience around them.

What happens after launch?

Your monthly care plan covers hosting, maintenance, security updates, and the level of content support included in your plan. There are no long-term contracts.
